# Voice

How everything we write sounds: AI replies (through the hotel's facts), website copy,
interface text, messages to hotels. Applies in Arabic and English.

## For guests

- Warm and respectful, never casual or salesy. Many guests are on a journey of faith.
- Short sentences. One idea each. Guests often read on a phone, in a second language.
- Say what happens next: "We confirm by WhatsApp", not "Submit".
- Facts only. If the hotel's facts don't say it, we don't say it. No invented reviews,
  ratings, counts or awards.
- Times as 24-hour (15:00). Prices as "SAR 650" in English and "650 ريال" in Arabic,
  with Western digits in both.
- Arabic is written as Arabic, not translated word for word from English. Use Modern
  Standard Arabic that a Saudi or Egyptian reader finds natural.

## For hotels (sales, onboarding, reports)

- Plain business language. Lead with the result for the hotel: fewer missed messages,
  more direct bookings, less commission.
- Numbers from the pilot report only. No promises we cannot measure.
- Free first: the pilot is free and ends without obligation. Say so clearly.

## Interface text

- Sentence case. Buttons name the action ("Send booking request").
- Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it, without apologising.
- No em dashes.
